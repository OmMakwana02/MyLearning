Parameter Guidelines

Temperature

Enter the current air temperature in Celsius. Higher temperatures increase fire risk.

Relative Humidity

Percentage of moisture in the air. Lower humidity increases fire risk.

Wind Speed

Wind speed in km/h. Higher wind speeds can spread fires more rapidly.

Rainfall

Recent rainfall in mm. More rain generally reduces fire risk.

FFMC

Fine Fuel Moisture Code (0-100). Lower values indicate drier fine fuels.

DMC

Duff Moisture Code (0-300). Measures moisture in deeper organic layers.

ISI

Initial Spread Index (0-50). Indicates how quickly a fire can spread.

Region

Geographic region affects fire behavior patterns and risk factors.
